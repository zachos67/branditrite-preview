Awards

Recognised for engineering excellence.

Sydney Engineering Excellence Awards (EEA) 2013

Highly Commended · Small Business Venture · September 2013

SignRISE received a “Highly Commended” recognition in the Small Business Venture category, which honours engineering excellence in organisations with fewer than 100 employees and less than $20 million turnover. The award recognised three product lines: the WondaSIGN 360° Events & Promotions range, the Expo Modular range, and Pop Up Car Roof Media.

Judges praised the designs for “strength of design, enabling excellence in promotion yet simplicity of use” — alongside product quality, ease of installation, multi-purpose functionality, stability, durability, backlighting, lightweight materials and usage flexibility.

“There can be no greater accolade for achievement than the recognition of peers' success as judged by fellow peers.” — John Nichols, EEA President

Terms & Conditions

Clear terms, covered.

A summary of the key terms — see the full Terms & Conditions for complete detail.

Warranty coverage

Two-year protection for hardware against manufacturing defects (excluding wear and tear). Fabrics have UV coating, though colour fastness isn't guaranteed due to environmental factors.

Warranty conditions

Normal use only, with proper SignRISE equipment and storage. Tampering or misuse voids coverage. Only authorised managers can approve claims and returns.

Returns & repairs

Contact us within 7 days for shipping damage and 30 days for standard warranty returns. Return shipping is prepaid by the customer.

Orders & production

Standard production ~14 days from artwork approval. Custom orders require full upfront payment; others a 50% deposit. Cancellations incur full material and labour charges.

Pricing

A built-in 10% discount applies for displaying the SignRISE logo (≈3cm × 7cm, bottom-right). Removing it adds a 10% surcharge.

Freight & collection

Freight charges excluded unless quoted separately. Products become company property if uncollected after 7 days.
